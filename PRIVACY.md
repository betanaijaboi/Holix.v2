# Privacy Policy — HOLIX Magazine

Last updated: September 1, 2026

## What we collect

- The name you enter to register as a member — no email or password is required.
- Comments you post on magazine issues.
- Basic usage data needed to run the site (e.g. which issues are viewed).

## Why we collect it

To identify you as a member within the library, attribute your comments, and operate the reading experience.

## Who we share it with

- **Supabase** — our database provider, storing member names, comments, and magazine data. See [Supabase's privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Your rights (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023)

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data (your member name and comments) at any time by contacting us below.

## Data retention

We keep your member record and comments until you request deletion.

## Security

Member identification is a lightweight name-based system, not a password account — there is no password to protect. Comments and member data are stored in Supabase with standard database security controls.
